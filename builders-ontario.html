The Two Pillars of the Ontario Builder Program

Combining temporary enhanced provincial tax recovery with exclusive institutional down payment capital.

Pillar 1 · Statutory Recovery

Ontario HST Rebate Advance

On newly constructed Ontario properties, owner-occupiers qualify for up to $130,000 in combined tax relief under temporary 2026 guidelines (featuring the Enhanced New Housing Rebate up to $80,000 + ONHAP up to $50,000). We structure and advance this statutory cash value directly at closing.

  • Advances up to $130,000 on the Statement of Adjustments
  • Open to first-time buyers, repeat move-up families, and individual rental investors
  • Coordinates directly with conveyancers to satisfy lender down-payment rules
Pillar 2 · Institutional Capital

Buy.ca 1.5% Direct Cash Bonus

Administered by Perch Capital, this highly exclusive program adds an extra 1.5% purchase cash bonus co-funded and credited directly onto the closing Statement of Adjustments.

  • Adds direct cash: e.g. $12,000 cash credit on an $800,000 home purchase
  • Combines with the HST rebate to establish a closing down-payment pool of 13% to 20% of the price
  • Allows qualified, strong-income buyers to close with as little as zero cash out-of-pocket

Case Study: Repeat Buyer (Non-FTHB) Rebate & Benefit Breakdown

A common misconception among sales agents is that buyers must be First-Time Home Buyers (FTHB) to qualify. Here is how the numbers break down for a repeat or move-up buyer on a newly built Ontario home:

Rebate / Benefit Program Eligible as Non-FTHB / Repeat Buyer? What It Does & Value
Federal FTHB GST/HST Rebate ❌ No Up to $50K; specifically first-time buyers only (phases out above $1M).
Existing Ontario New Housing Rebate ✅ Yes Up to $24K provincial HST rebate (75% of the 8% provincial tax, no upper purchase price cap).
Ontario Enhanced New Housing Rebate (ENHR) ✅ Yes Tops the Ontario relief up to 100% of the 8% provincial HST, max $80K (for homes under $1.85M).
Ontario New Home Affordability Payment (ONHAP) ✅ Yes Up to $50K, effectively covering up to 100% of the 5% federal HST portion (reduced by any federal rebate received).
