Up to $50,000 Relief

100% GST Rebate for First-Time Buyers

Under official CRA rules, eligible first-time home buyers qualify for a 100% rebate of the 5% federal GST on new builds up to $1,000,000 (phasing out between $1M and $1.5M).

  • Saves up to $50,000 in federal tax on new construction
  • Available across Alberta, Ontario, and all provinces
  • Must be occupied as your primary place of residence
Advance at Closing

Bridging Your Down Payment Gap

Normally, builder show-home contracts simply reduce the sticker price, leaving you to supply 100% of your down payment from savings. We structure the rebate so funds arrive on possession day.

  • Rebates advanced directly onto the Statement of Adjustments
  • Reduces liquid cash required to close the purchase
  • Prevents last-minute scrambling for parental gift funds
Repeat Buyers Included

You Do NOT Have to Be a First-Time Buyer

Repeat and move-up buyers still qualify for baseline tax rebates! In Ontario, buyers get 75% of the provincial HST back up to $24,000 with NO upper purchase price limit.

  • Ontario: Full $24,000 provincial rebate on any price point
  • Alberta: Up to $6,300 federal GST rebate under $450k
  • Individual rental investors qualify under separate CRA rules
