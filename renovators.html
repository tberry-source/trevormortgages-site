Project Capabilities

3 Specialized Renovation Refinance Scenarios

How we tailor the borrowing architecture based on the specific scope your client wants to execute.

High-Yield Suites

Legal Basement & Detached Suites

Building a legal suite or a garden garage carriage house is incredibly profitable. I structure equity refinancing so that up to 50%–100% of the future projected rental revenue ($1,400 to $2,000+/mo) can be added to their household income to help them qualify for the mortgage.

Immediate Purchases

Purchase Plus Improvements (PPI)

When a buyer finds a fixer-upper but lacks the cash to renovate, standard lenders decline them. I secure approval to roll up to $40,000 to $100,000+ of your renovation quote directly into their new home purchase mortgage before possession.

Existing Equity

Refinance Plus Improvements (RPI)

For homeowners with established equity, we replace their existing mortgage with an expanded, low-interest facility. This releases construction milestone cash directly to your business draw account based on completed progress inspections—keeping your trades paid and materials ordered on schedule.
