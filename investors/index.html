Exit & Takeout Strategy

Think beyond the closing date

For short-term capital, the exit is part of the financing plan from the start. Sell, refinance or hold, the next step should be clear before the money goes in.

  • Sell after renovation
  • Refinance into a rental hold
  • Alternative or conventional takeout
  • Equity recycling into the next deal

Common investor scenarios

Not every investor deal looks the same.

Purchase, renovate, refinance or hold. The right structure depends on the property, the borrower, the timeline and the exit.

Flip

Buy, renovate and sell. Structure acquisition and renovation financing around speed, leverage and a clear sale exit.

BRRRR

Buy, renovate, rent and refinance. Plan the front-end financing with the long-term takeout strategy in mind.

Rental & Portfolio

Acquire or refinance buy-and-hold properties, from the next rental to a growing portfolio with the right lender fit.

Development / Complex Deals

Infill, conversions, small residential development and transactions that need a more customized financing structure.
