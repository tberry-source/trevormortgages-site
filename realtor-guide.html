Common Misconception: Do Buyers Need to Be First-Time Buyers?

No. Any eligible Canadian purchaser (repeat buyers, move-up families, downsizers, or relocations) buying a newly constructed home as their primary residence qualifies for statutory GST/HST new housing rebates.

Standard Baseline Rebate (All Buyers):

Available to all buyers. In Alberta, covers 36% of GST up to $350k ($6,300 max, phasing out at $450k). In Ontario, covers 75% of provincial HST up to $24,000 with NO purchase price cap.

Enhanced FTHB Top-Up (First-Time Only):

Specifically for first-time buyers. Provides a 100% rebate of the 5% GST on new homes up to $1,000,000 (up to $50,000 maximum), phasing out linearly up to $1.5M.

Practical Deal Mechanics

The 3-Step Advance & Payoff Lifecycle

A missing $15,000 on possession day can unravel months of hard work. Here is the exact closing sequence showing how the builder assigns credit entitlement, how funds are advanced to the lawyer at closing, and how CRA proceeds pay off the advance.

Step 01 · Contract Stage

1. Builder Credit Assignment

In the purchase contract, the builder assigns the rebate and credit entitlement to the buyer rather than locking it into a generic price discount.

  • The buyer retains entitlement to the statutory federal and provincial tax rebate
  • Purchase agreement language is pre-cleared for lender underwriting approval
  • Eliminates confusion between builder sticker price and actual buyer closing cash
Step 02 · Possession Day

2. Closing Funds Advanced

On closing day, we advance the approved rebate amount directly to the conveyancing real estate lawyer's trust account to satisfy the down payment.

  • Funds arrive directly on the lawyer's Statement of Adjustments as buyer down payment
  • Satisfies lender minimum equity thresholds so mortgage conditions clear
  • The builder gets paid 100% of their contract purchase funds with zero closing delay
Step 03 · Post-Possession

3. CRA Submission & Payoff

The appropriate CRA/Ontario rebate application is completed based on occupancy and transaction type to claim the government refund.

  • We supply the complete paperwork package and documentation checklist for the CRA
  • When the CRA issues the rebate refund cheque or direct deposit, it pays off the advance
  • Buyer gets into the home today without liquidating emergency cash or investments

Capitalizing The Tax Credit

Standard Builder Price Discount vs. The Down Payment Bonus

Most buyers and agents don't realize that standard builder rebate assignment doesn't solve the down payment barrier. Here is how specialized advance financing changes the equation.

Traditional Builder Practice

The Standard Builder Assignment

When a buyer signs a standard builder contract stating "Price includes GST/HST with rebate assigned to builder," the builder simply takes the tax credit to offset their gross sticker price.

  • The Problem: The buyer still has to come up with 100% of their 5% to 20% down payment from their own bank account.
  • Cash-to-Close Crunch: Legal adjustments, land transfer taxes, and closing fees still have to be paid out-of-pocket on closing day.
  • Deal Risk: Buyers with strong income but modest savings walk away from show homes because they cannot hit deposit milestones.
Specialized Lending Program

The Down Payment Bonus Advance

Through specialized proprietary lending structures (available for qualifying new construction in Ontario), the value of the tax rebate is paired with partner cashback and advanced directly at closing.

  • Funds the Down Payment: Advances between 13% and 20% of the purchase price directly on the Statement of Adjustments.
  • Available to Repeat Buyers & Investors: Not limited to first-time buyers; applies to any residential property of 1 to 4 units.
  • Closes Stalled Deals: Allows buyers who are $15,000–$50,000+ short on cash-to-close to remove financing conditions and close.
