Strategic Milestones

When to Bring Trevor into Your Planning Process

Key indicators where structuring a low-cost debt facility makes strategic sense before executing your planning changes.

1. Business Owner Needs Personal Capital

Before creating a significant personal distribution solely to fund a purchase or expense, see what financing options may be available to avoid tax-heavy brackets.

2. Selling Investments for Property Purchase

Compare mortgage or home equity financing before liquidating a taxable portfolio, preserving compound growth and avoiding immediate capital gains.

3. Mortgage-Free or High-Equity Client

Explore whether existing, dormant home equity could provide a tax-efficient, non-registered source of wealth building or backup reserves.

4. Investor Acquiring Another Property

Review how existing equity, re-advanceable structures, and current financing can support the next acquisition without compromising personal cash limits.

5. Approaching Retirement

Compare equity-access structures with registered or investment withdrawals to optimize cash flow and protect long-term retirement liquidity.

6. Complex Corporate Income

Review self-employed lender options and Holdco programs before altering salary or dividend allocations solely to pass mortgage qualification checks.
