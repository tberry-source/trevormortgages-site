The Smith Manoeuvre

Re-Advanceable Debt Implementation

We structure re-advanceable mortgage and HELOC facilities that automatically convert every dollar of principal paid on their residential mortgage into available credit for non-registered investment contributions. This establishes a continuous loop of tax deductions that can be re-invested into your portfolios.

  • Accelerate mortgage payoff while growing non-registered assets
  • Converts expensive consumer debt into tax-deductible investment capital
  • Fully compliant, structured leverage aligned with the CFP planning process

Retirement Cash-Flow Protection

Client Lender Fit & Qualification Classifier

Run high-level scenarios to instantly pre-screen what tier of financing, pricing, and underwriter guidelines fit your client's current balance sheet profile.

1. Credit Score Profile

Select the credit rating tier matching the primary applicant's history.

Excellent (720+)
Clean historical repayment. Top tier A-lender bank eligibility.
Good (650 - 719)
Standard credit history. Clean access to monoline wholesale prime options.
Fair (580 - 649)
Minor late payments, credit utilization, or past consumer proposal.
Challenged (< 580)
Active collections, severe rating hits, or bankruptcy. Requires asset focus.

2. Income Verification Method

Select how the household income is declared and underwritten.

Standard T4 Salaried
Consistent salaried employment, verifiable via standard job letter & paystubs.
Self-Employed (BFS)
Sole proprietor or incorporated professional utilizing legitimate tax write-offs.
Retired / Wealth Assets
Relying on CPP, OAS, corporate dividends, RRIF drawdowns, or liquid assets.
Holding Co / Complex
Inter-company loans, rental portfolios, or active corporate balance sheets.

3. Available Equity / Down Payment Position

Select the loan-to-value (LTV) structure or available cash assets.

20%+ Equity / Down
Conventional positioning. No mandatory CMHC default insurance. Max flexibility.
Under 20% (High Ratio)
Insured mortgage structure. Capped guidelines and requires 25-yr amortization max.

4. Active CRA Balances or Unsecured Debt

Are there outstanding personal/corporate tax liabilities or extreme credit card debt?

Fully Clear / Standard
CRA accounts fully paid up-to-date. Normal consumer debt carrying ratios.
Active CRA/Tax Arrears
Outstanding tax notices, corporate source deductions, or severe debt load.
