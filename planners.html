The Smith Manoeuvre

Re-Advanceable Debt Implementation

We structure re-advanceable mortgage and HELOC facilities that automatically convert every dollar of principal paid on their residential mortgage into available credit for non-registered investment contributions. This establishes a continuous loop of tax deductions that can be re-invested into your portfolios.

  • Accelerate mortgage payoff while growing non-registered assets
  • Converts expensive consumer debt into tax-deductible investment capital
  • Fully compliant, structured leverage aligned with the CFP planning process
Retirement Cash-Flow

Retirement Cash-Flow Protection

Help pre-retirees and retirees restructure debt to minimize mandatory monthly carrying costs. Tapping equity strategically avoids forced asset liquidations in down market cycles, protecting the longevity of their portfolio assets under management.

  • Minimize carrying costs to optimize retirement income
  • Mitigate sequence-of-returns risk during market corrections
  • Maintain assets under management cleanly
Tax Optimization

Down Payment Liquidity Planning

Optimize when and how clients extract equity or use home-buyer incentives without triggering unwanted capital gains in their taxable investment portfolios. We design a leverage framework that satisfies down payment requirements while preserving your investment strategy.
