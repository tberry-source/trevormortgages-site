The Mechanics

How We Capitalize the GST Rebate at Closing

A missing $15,000 on possession day can unravel months of construction. Here is the compliant 3-step operational flow we use to bridge the buyer's cash gap.

Step 01 · Underwriting & Contract

1. Structure the Credit

During contract drafting, we ensure the purchase agreement is drawn up so the buyer's statutory rebate entitlement can be properly capitalized, rather than locked up as a passive builder subsidy.

  • Pre-cleared contract compliance ensures lender approval
  • Applies to qualifying new builds under $1.5 million
  • Coordinates directly with your sales and conveyancing teams
Step 02 · Closing Day Advance

2. Bridge the Cash-to-Close

On possession day, we advance the approved federal GST rebate amount (up to $50,000) directly to the buyer's real estate lawyer's trust account to satisfy the cash-to-close ledger.

  • Appears on the Statement of Adjustments as buyer equity
  • Satisfies lender minimum down payment thresholds
  • The builder gets paid 100% of their contract purchase funds with zero delay
Step 03 · Post-Closing CRA Payoff

3. CRA Submission & Payoff

Immediately following possession, the buyer submits their statutory rebate application to the CRA. When the government issues the refund cheque, those proceeds pay off the closing-day advance.

  • Full paperwork preparation and documentation provided to the buyer
  • The buyer gets into the home today without draining emergency reserves
  • Clean, transparent transaction lifecycle from contract to possession

Program Parameters

Alberta GST Rebate Rules & Caps

This statutory program is subject to strict CRA guidelines. Here are the key thresholds we pre-underwrite for your buyers:

First-Time Home Buyers (FTHB)

Eligible first-time buyers recover 100% of the 5% federal GST on new homes up to a purchase price of $1,000,000.

  • Maximum statutory rebate value of $50,000
  • Phases out linearly between $1,000,000 and $1,500,000
  • Must occupy the property as their primary place of residence

Repeat & Move-Up Buyers

Buyers who have owned a home previously do not qualify for the first-time top-up, but still access the baseline CRA statutory GST rebate.

  • Standard rebate of 36% of the 5% GST on homes up to $350k
  • Maximum standard rebate value of $6,300
  • Phases out linearly between $350,000 and $450,000
